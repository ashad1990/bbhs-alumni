A network grows through people who know their classmates.

## One or two volunteers per batch

We propose inviting one or two representatives from each graduation batch. They would help classmates hear about the network, share updates, and suggest activities.

Representatives do not need to manage a database or collect payments. Their role is to help people connect.

## Respect privacy

Only publish a representative's name and contact link with their permission. Never publish registration responses or a private list of alumni.

**The directory currently contains sample entries and open positions, not appointed representatives.**
