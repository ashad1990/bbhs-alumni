We may live in different places and work in different fields, but we share a beginning: Blue Bird High School and College in Sylhet.

## Start with connection

This alumni network is a proposal to help former students find one another, exchange opportunities, and stay connected to their school community.

The first step is simple: invite classmates to reconnect. Registration will be through a free Google Form once the association publishes its real form link.

## Keep it useful

Career conversations, business connections, reunions, sports, and social activities can make this a community that stays active all year.

**This is sample launch content, not an announcement of an established or officially endorsed association.**
